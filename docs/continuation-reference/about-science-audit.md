## Actual equations and important omissions

**Direct-dough yeast (utils.ts:65–98, 167–202).** For positive hours, RT estimate is `9.5 / (h^1.65 × 2.5^((T−25)/10) × tropical)`, with tropical=1 below 30°C, 1.15 at 30–<33°C, and 1.25 at ≥33°C. It rejects very long warm combinations (≥30h at ≥28°C or ≥36h at ≥25°C). Cold estimate is `7.5 / (max(1,h)^1.313 × 2^((Tf−4)/10))`. About omits the tropical divisor from its displayed RT equation, although nearby text mentions it.

If cold>0 and RT≤4h, use only cold estimate with a 0.05% floor. Otherwise with cold>0, use weights `RT/max(rtDose,0.05)` and `cold/coldDose` and the weighted average, again floored at 0.05%. Pure RT uses RT estimate with the same floor. Flavor priority multiplies by 0.7, speed by 1.8, then cap at 1.5% and round to four decimals. These policy adjustments and floor materially affect the final “precise quantity” and should be disclosed. The floor can be reduced afterward by flavor priority, so it is not an invariant final lower bound.

**Preferments (data.ts:915–1001).** Preferment flour is rounded from overall flour × selected percentage; water is rounded using the type's fixed hydration. Main flour/water subtract those quantities. Biga is forced cold. Actual preferment hours are clamped by protocol. The same base laws receive correction factors: biga 2.2, fridge poolish 1.65, RT poolish 1/3.1, with 0.05% floor. When whole-dough target is supplied, seed dose is the greater of peak dose and `target/G`, where `G=2^(hours/tD)` and `tD=22.1×2^(-(T−6)/10)`. Dose is rounded to 0.1g before type conversion. About omits this growth model and max-dose rule. Matching a few asserted calibration points does not prove unrestricted exponential growth or guarantee that increasing seed still preserves the selected preferment peak time.

**Water calculation (utils.ts:529–541, 599–621).** Water heat capacity is 4180 and dry flour 1800 J/kg·K. The algorithm calculates capacity-weighted dry ingredients, preferment and free water, solves for water temperature after subtracting mixer rise, clamps water to 2–40°C, then calculates achievable dough temperature. It treats salt with dry-flour heat capacity and omits a detailed model for every enriched ingredient. About should describe a simplified heat balance, distinguish calculated from measured FDT, and encourage checking the mixed dough with a thermometer. Professional guidance supports measurement and mixer calibration. [King Arthur dough temperature](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/pro/reference/dough-temperature)

**Starter base curve (FermentChart.tsx:113–130).** `peakHours=clamp(5.5×2.8^((24−T)/10),1.75,24)`, with further caller adjustments. Document feed-ratio/maturity assumptions rather than treating this as a universal starter clock. Laboratory growth-rate measurements are not direct validation of this rise-time equation.

**Cooling limitation (AboutClient.tsx:472).** Page says target fridge temperature is reached within 1h. The inspected yeast functions do not integrate a one-hour cooling ramp; they receive total RT and cold hours. Therefore neither the broad “handles transitions” claim nor this exact one-hour statement is substantiated by these functions. Further scheduler tracing may reveal a separate allocation, but actual core cooling remains unvalidated here.

## Proposed About structure for alignment

1. What the app supports: specific doughs, preferments and supported temperature ranges.
2. How ingredient accounting works, including total flour and water in preferments.
3. How schedules and yeast estimates are generated, with preset scoring explicitly identified.
4. How water temperature is estimated and where measured dough temperature matters.
5. Evidence table linking each model/constant to an original source, calibration case or explicitly unvalidated assumption.
6. Limits and readiness cues, including sourdough variation and slow refrigeration transitions.

Apply the same corrections to English and French. No final replacement marketing copy should be approved until model scope and evidence are agreed with the owner.
