pizza preparation/shopping, navigation, sharing, yeast identification and learning help.

Criterion: labels identify the control; supporting text must add a decision,
consequence, recovery action or necessary qualification. Repeated labels,
unsupported superlatives and generic reassurance were removed.

  preserved practical observations and recipe-specific instructions.
