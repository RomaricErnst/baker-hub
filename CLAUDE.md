## Calculation and display conventions

### Commercial yeast
One model (`app/utils/yeastModel.ts`, 19 Nov 2026; method, data, constants and residuals in `docs/YEAST-MODEL.md`) sets every commercial-yeast dose and timing: direct dough, cold retard, hot kitchens, poolish/biga, the inverse for a baker-entered amount and the poolish peak. Instant yeast % of the fermenting flour = A · M · R · Heq^−b, A 1.41, b 1.15 (Phase 3 refit, 20 Nov 2026: 157 rows, 28 lineages, mixed effects with per-source offsets, credibility × regime weights; `docs/yeast-model/fit3/`); Heq = ∫ r(T(t)) dt, equivalent hours at 25 °C over the dough's temperature path (`doughThermalPath` in utils.ts: leaves the bowl at the target dough temperature, relaxes to the kitchen, cools into the fridge as the bulk or as pieces, warms back; τ from `thermalTauH`); r = Rosso CTMI relative to 25 °C with Tmax 45.4 (Salvadó 2011), Topt 40 (fitted, at its bound) and Tmin −3.2 (fitted; LOW CONFIDENCE); M = 1 salted dough, 0.70 poolish, 1.55 biga (fitted); R = rise target, 1 (doubled, E1), easing to 0.76 when the dough ends between 25 and 28 °C (E3, fitted). E2: the fridge mostly holds (r(6 °C) ≈ 0.11), the dough finishes at room temperature. It replaced Craig's RT law, the separate cold law, the fridge Q10, the tropical ÷1.15/÷1.25, the poolish ÷3.1/×1.65, biga ×2.2 and the leavening top-up. No salt or hydration factor (no source); the enriched rule (≥10 % sugar → fixed 1 % IDY) and the >5 % sugar osmotic note are kept as implemented; fresh ×3, active dry ×1.33 (MB6 KM p. xi). The constants change only with a refit (`docs/yeast-model/fit3/`, pre-registered registry and hold-out) and `tests/yeast-model.test.cjs` (constants ≡ doc ≡ About page, data-fit bounds, smoothness 2–40 °C / 1–96 h / fridge 1–10 °C, reference recipes). Distinguish extending a bake with cold time from replacing room-temperature time at a fixed bake time: the budget is NOT monotone in the window (a plan long enough to take the cold retard ferments less than a slightly shorter room-temperature one). Display the converted amount for the selected yeast. The 0.5 g threshold flags weighing difficulty; the automatic dose stays within 0.05–1.5 % IDY before an explicit override.

### Schedule
Single-phase, or two-phase tropical when window ≥16 h AND kitchen ≥28 °C.
15-min rounding · blocker edges EXCLUSIVE (> and <, never >= <=) · default fridge 6 °C · default bake 18h.
Final fridge exit (two-phase, 18 Nov 2026 retention round 4): free plans keep the engine's exit (longest final proof; sweep: 0 of 10,780 no-blocker plans changed). (1) When the exit must fall in busy time, it is the LATEST workable one (warm-up + shortest final proof before the preheat, e.g. 6:45 for a 9:15 bake after nights to 7:00), or the first free quarter if one exists; an exit the baker arranged for this exact bake (`fridgeRemoval` {at, bake}) is kept, so saved bakes are never re-timed. (2) Bake again carries the previous plan's exit-to-bake lead (`planExitLead` → session `exitLead`, `app/utils/exitLead.ts`, same kitchen temp and style only) as `buildSchedule(…, exitLeadMin)`: used only when that exit is free and inside [longest, shortest] proof bounds, so a later bake extends the cold spell instead of the proof (Thomas: 11:00 bake → out 8:00, 3 h as last week). Every planner input (`ScheduleDraftInput`, `EditInput`, repair, yeast inversion, bake-from-fridge) passes it. (3) A longer preheat chosen on the plan (`preheatExtraMin`) moves only the Preheat step: the dough is planned with `planPreheatMin` (the oven's own) and `withLongerPreheat` moves the preheat action (page schedule and `assessScheduleDraft`). The "Before your recipe" sheet states a clash and another live bake's oven time (`ovenShare`, plural subject from `bakeSubject`) in one sentence; its suggestions avoid that oven time and Keep also keeps the oven (`onOvenKept`). Tests: `tests/retention-round4-engine.test.cjs`, `tests/mobile/retention-round4-engine.spec.cjs`.

### DDT
The water-temperature balance includes flour, starter or preferment, thermal input from kneading, and the heat of melting ice. A levain's flour and water are already inside the dough's totals; in the balance they sit at the starter's temperature: room temperature after a feed, the warmed temperature (`prefTempAfterWarmup` from fridge to kitchen over the time out) when a fridge starter goes into the dough without another feed, or the baker's measured temperature (Fine-tune), which always wins (`prefTempForBalance` in page.tsx). Use the calculated result for the specific formula. In Simple, kneading is assumed by hand and never mentioned on the recipe (12 Nov 2026: the mixer question under Water was removed; a mixer would add only a few degrees of friction, and Custom exposes equipment explicitly). The water line (`waterGuideLine`) says how to reach the temperature without a thermometer — room temperature, a fridge/room split, lukewarm, or ice — and offers "how to get it ▾" only when there are steps (ice, or a measured water source).

### Preferments
Poolish RT 6–14 h, fridge ≤24 h · Biga fridge ≤72 h · Levain = sourdough only.
Preferment doses (yeast model, 19 Nov 2026): a poolish or biga holds the peak dose of its OWN path (`prefermentPath` in data.ts: made at the kitchen temperature; a fridge poolish out for its warm-up before the mix, a room poolish chilled for its cool-down; medium factor M), capped 0.05–1.5 %; the final dough gets its own yeast, the direct-dough need of the dough's path from the mix to the bake (`finalYeastGrams`, always present with a preferment; Modernist's poolish doughs total 0.4–1.9× that need, geometric mean 1.16; no credit for the preferment's yeast; preferments above ~35 % of the flour are outside that evidence). `recipe.yeast` is that final-dough need (the old `yeastReduction` is no longer applied). A made preferment stays valid when only the dough's path moves (`madeFormulaMatches` compares the preferment). Poolish near its peak at the mix (18 Nov 2026, owner; Thomas, 20 °C, 11 h poolish held the whole dough's yeast and peaked ~4.5 h in): the test is the model read backwards (`poolishPeakHours` inverts the peak dose of `computePrefermentRecipe` on the same path; pass `massG` and the conditioning hours): a poolish dosed for its window peaks at the mix by construction, so the levers below act only when the dose is held at the 0.05 % floor or the window outruns the dosed one (`preferment.pastPeak`, which the page's `poolishPeakAtMix` reads). Past its peak when it peaks more than `poolishPastPeakToleranceH` before the mix: usable until peak + max(`rtTolUpper` 0.75–2 h, ⅓ of its time to peak) at room temperature (fond.kitchen: a 12 h poolish is "past peak but usable" to 14–16 h; Suas's 12–15 h span), peak + 12 h in the fridge (The Dough Formula; fond: a ripe poolish holds ~24 h cold) (19 Nov 2026 recheck; the books give a visual cue only, no hours). Only then, levers the baker left to the planner (`app/utils/poolishRipeness.ts`, judged by the page's `poolishPeakAtMix` = the recipe the plan would show): (2) fridge, only with storage 'auto', in the fridge protocol's window (≤24 h, warm-up kept); (3) a later poolish start, same mix, only without a set poolish time, within the storage's own window (never below it); (4) else the plan stands (the final dough's yeast line is always there; whole-dough row, shopping and mixing batches use `recipeYeastTotal`). The poolish % is never changed. Plans that pass are returned as found (no extra fields). Paths: `commercialRecommendation`, the kept-storage replan (`ripenFixedPoolish`), Restart options, and the edit preview (`ripenEdit`). Tests: `tests/poolish-peak.test.cjs`, `tests/mobile/poolish-peak.spec.cjs`.

### Baker overrides (Custom)
A baker-entered yeast amount (direct commercial dough) re-times the plan: `fermentationHoursForYeast` inverts the same budget (`fermentationBudget` on `doughThermalPath`) through `buildSchedule`, and the solver, readiness window and chart use that window. The budget is not monotone across the room/cold switch, so the inversion scans 0.5 h steps for every window where the budget rises through the target and returns the one nearest `nearH` (default the style's coldH + rtH) within `maxH` (the time left: `checkYeastEntry`, the picker's `yeastTimingFor`); the readiness bounds use `nearH` = the centre. Never display an entered amount on a schedule timed for another. Poolish storage can be fixed to fridge or room; 'auto' compares both. Plan-input edits made on other steps reach the scheduler through the page's `planReplanPending` flag, not a change-only effect (the scheduler is unmounted on those steps). A baker-entered starter % (levain) scales the room-temperature bulk by `starterBulkFactor` (Craig's regression against the engine's reference % for the kitchen; exactly 1 without a choice). Every schedule build (`buildSchedule`, draft validation, repair search) and the sourdough solver windows (`starterBulkDeltaH`) must add the same hours. Cold phases are not scaled.

Only one step is mounted at a time, so there is never a second solver instance. Keep starter state lifted in the page.

### Sourdough solver (SchedulePicker)
Isolated from non-sourdough engine (early return). Candidate pools Peak1/2A/2B/fridge-hold, blocker-aware,
fridge revival biology, event-driven chart, traffic-light pills (no red in starter card).
The starter ratio multiplier uses 1 + 0.5 × ln(feed ratio). Keep scoring, card and curve on the same peak estimate.
Compute style defaults LOCALLY inside the solver (stale `_sfDef` closures caused real bugs).

**Peak-model invariant (learned July 2026):** every candidate family must derive ONE peak.
Each family produces a *scoring* peak (drives the green/yellow pill, in candidate generation)
and a *display* peak (chart bell `bellPeakTime` + card "Peak around…"/PEAK row). They MUST be
equal. They drifted because paths applied different stretch to `adjPeakH` (bare vs
`× _refreshStretchFactor` vs `× _preMixStretchFactor`) → scoring more optimistic than the bell
→ FALSE GREEN (starter "ready at mix" while still rising). Every family, peak2A included, now
scores through `computeStarterPeakMs(feedMs, refPeak, adjPeakH)`, the same helper and reference
the pre-mix bell uses. `tests/roundtrip/peak-consistency.mjs` (CI) sweeps 384 temperature ×
location × last-fed × horizon × Nights cases and asserts card PEAK ≡ active bell and that green
means mix within the solver's tolerance of the shown peak. The trace's `scoredPeakMs` may differ
from the shown peak when the render re-aligns a feed onto the mix; the pill is judged on the shown peak.

**Fridge-kept starter, distant bake (11 Nov 2026):** `deriveStarterPeakTime` starts the revival in the run-up (cycles + dough + 12 h) when that leaves more than 24 h before it (16 Nov 2026; was 96 h), at a time clear of busy periods (earlier first, then later within the 12 h margin), with the cycles of the age the starter will have then (`starterAgeAt`: it ages in the fridge); `fridge-starter.mjs` asserts no feed in the first 48 h for 216–240 h bakes. Feed labels: the first planned feed is never "intermediate" (`starterFeedLabelFor`).

**After-work levain, cool kitchen (17 Nov 2026):** only where the established search finds no plan for the requested bake; plans it finds stay byte-identical (sweep: free and nights-only cases ≡ main).
(1) `searchStarterCandidate(…, ratios=true)` (from `replanStarter`): pass A is the established search. ## UX Principles
- Empathetic companion: no red text, no ⚠️, warnings are observations. Info only when actionable.
- Water source-agnostic. "Make Poolish" / "Start Dough" everywhere. FR levain: Rafraîchi/Rafraîchir (never Repas/Nourrir), Pétrissage.
- EN 12 h clock · FR 24 h (18h30) and FR weekdays (dim. lun. …) — formatTime(d, locale).
- Mobile first: instant scrolls only; nothing fixed over the bottom nav; visualViewport guard when keyboard open; deferred date-picker apply on coarse pointers; keep tab content mounted (display:none) — never use timed toasts for hidden-tab changes.
- No step numbers on StepCard (nor in Preparation's progress line). bakeType-aware copy (bread uses bread language). Internal mode strings: 'simple' | 'custom'.
- Bake day (17 Nov 2026 retention round 2): the guide's Now/Next mark (`guideMark`) reads done-ness from state (`stepIsDone`), never from the previous render's DOM, so a tick moves the bar and "Go to: …" at once; the oven (preheat, bake) is part of the clock, and Preparation's forward action is "Baking & serving →" once every Preparation step is done or the oven's time has come. A recorded fact (mix, preferment, feed) is never "You changed your choices": it does not set `protocolStale`, never routes into setup, and a remaining plan that no longer fits is one line in Preparation (`executionClash`, beside Adjust timing). Guide ticks are keyed by the bake's journal id (`progressScope`; the schedule snapshot only without one) and follow their step by title when the plan inserts or drops steps (`remapCompletedSteps`). `journalId` is React state set in the same update as a restored bake; switching flushes the bake on screen into its entry first.
