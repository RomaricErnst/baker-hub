## Calculation and display conventions

### Commercial yeast
One model (`app/utils/yeastModel.ts`, 19 Nov 2026; method, data, constants and residuals in `docs/YEAST-MODEL.md`) sets every commercial-yeast dose and timing: direct dough, cold retard, hot kitchens, poolish/biga, the inverse for a baker-entered amount and the poolish peak. Instant yeast % of the fermenting flour = A · M · R · Heq^−b, A 1.41, b 1.15 (Phase 3 refit, 20 Nov 2026: 157 rows, 28 lineages, mixed effects with per-source offsets, credibility × regime weights; `docs/yeast-model/fit3/`); Heq = ∫ r(T(t)) dt, equivalent hours at 25 °C over the dough's temperature path (`doughThermalPath` in utils.ts: leaves the bowl at the target dough temperature, relaxes to the kitchen, cools into the fridge as the bulk or as pieces, warms back; τ from `thermalTauH`); r = Rosso CTMI relative to 25 °C with Tmax 45.4 (Salvadó 2011), Topt 40 (fitted, at its bound) and Tmin −3.2 (fitted; LOW CONFIDENCE); M = 1 salted dough, 0.70 poolish, 1.55 biga (fitted); R = rise target, 1 (doubled, E1), easing to 0.76 when the dough ends between 25 and 28 °C (E3, fitted). E2: the fridge mostly holds (r(6 °C) ≈ 0.11), the dough finishes at room temperature. It replaced Craig's RT law, the separate cold law, the fridge Q10, the tropical ÷1.15/÷1.25, the poolish ÷3.1/×1.65, biga ×2.2 and the leavening top-up. No salt or hydration factor (no source); the enriched rule (≥10 % sugar → fixed 1 % IDY) and the >5 % sugar osmotic note are kept as implemented; fresh ×3, active dry ×1.33 (MB6 KM p. xi). The constants change only with a refit (`docs/yeast-model/fit3/`, pre-registered registry and hold-out) and `tests/yeast-model.test.cjs` (constants ≡ doc ≡ About page, data-fit bounds, smoothness 2–40 °C / 1–96 h / fridge 1–10 °C, reference recipes). Distinguish extending a bake with cold time from replacing room-temperature time at a fixed bake time: the budget is NOT monotone in the window (a plan long enough to take the cold retard ferments less than a slightly shorter room-temperature one). Display the converted amount for the selected yeast. The 0.5 g threshold flags weighing difficulty; the automatic dose stays within 0.05–1.5 % IDY before an explicit override. A direct dough with more than 8 h at room temperature and no cold phase (Custom) carries one note, "Beyond 8h at room temperature, check the dough regularly." (`poolishSuggestion`; 21 Nov 2026 round 8, S4: its second sentence, "A poolish needs its own preparation schedule", offered nothing to act on and is gone). The Recipe's folded "Alternative method for this schedule" card ("Poolish preferment recommended. For a poolish, use the flour, water and yeast amounts shown…") is gone too (round 9 S1: a direct recipe shows no poolish amounts; `recipeOutput.poolishTitle/Body` removed).

### Flour strength (21 Nov 2026, owner)
Hydration follows the flour TYPE only (`FLOUR_DATA.hydrationDelta`); W and protein never change it (W is an alveograph value at fixed water and does not predict absorption: Pasini Verde W295 58.8 % vs Marrone W390 58.7 %). Fermentation tolerance (`fermToleranceMultiplier`, the page's `flourStrength`) is the type's value, except for a white-wheat type (`W_TOLERANCE_FLOURS`: pizza00, strong00, bread, allpurpose, manitoba) whose W is STATED (`wIsStated`: w source 'manual', 'photo', or 'exact' = a product's published W): then `ftmFromW(W)` (data.ts; piecewise linear (130,0.75) (200,0.90) (250,1.00) (300,1.20) (360,1.35), flat outside, rounded to 0.01) REPLACES it (`flourTolerance`, never multiplied). Rye, wholemeal and semolina keep their type. Blends weigh each component's own tolerance; flour 1's W is `w1` only (a legacy `wOverride` may be a blended W and is never read as stated). Catalogue tiles (`flourBehaviour`): an unmapped type (Italian 00/0/1…) is pizza00 unless its W is published and ≥ 270 (strong00); an estimated W no longer crosses to strong00 (Caputo Pizzeria/Nuvola/Cuoco… → pizza00: tolerance 1.2 → 1.0, hydration −2). Saved blends keep their stored tile. The tolerance scales coldH/preferredColdH, the fridge-poolish window and the readiness plateau (SchedulePicker), and the bulk cue text (weak flour "holds less — end the bulk at a smaller rise", never "ferments faster"). It does NOT set the levain % (21 Nov 2026 round 8, research verdict): the `starterBand × ftm` of 24 May 2026 had no source; no study or professional text ties inoculation to flour strength (Katina 2004/2006, Gocmen 2007, Seis Subaşı & Ercan 2024, Cardinali 2023, Clarke 2002/2004, Hamelman: strength sets how long a dough holds, time is the lever; rye wants more prefermented flour, the multiplier pointed the other way), so `referenceStarterPct(T)`, `sourdoughGuidance`, `starterBulkFactor(pct, T)`, `raisedStarterPcts(T)` and `raisedLevainFits(flour, T, feedToMix, pct, ratios)` take no flour and are the same for every flour. Nor does it touch the starter's ready band (21 Nov 2026, round 8 follow-up): the solver's green tolerance was `baseTOL × ftm` (and `knownPeakMixUsable`'s), so a strong dough flour called a starter 4.3 h before its peak green; a starter's readiness is its own jar's, no source ties it to the dough's flour, so solver green ≡ strip ready band (`readyBand`) ≡ `mixNearPeak` for every flour, and `peak-consistency.mjs` checks it strictly at flour strengths 0.75/1/1.35. Rye/wholemeal get the general band: a rye-specific inoculation is an open question, not a rule. Tests: `tests/levain-flour-independent.test.cjs`; `peak-consistency.mjs` sweeps strengths 0.75/1/1.35; `levain-raise.mjs` runs the 19 °C weekday evening at 0.75 and 1.35. It does NOT touch the commercial yeast at a fixed schedule: the ÷ tolerance in `calculateRecipe` and the × tolerance on an entered amount (`yeastTimingFor`, `checkYeastEntry`) are gone (double count; `docs/YEAST-MODEL.md` §8.4). Texts: About "The flour", "Why this value?" (flour type + one line on W, the W line only for a chosen flour), the manual flour form. One sum (22 Nov 2026 round 9 A): `recommendedHydration(style, oven, kitchen °C, humidity, blend)` in utils.ts (style + oven (half for enriched/oily styles) + kitchen ±2 + flour type, never below the style's floor) is what `calculateRecipe` uses and what Fine-tune's hint and "Why this value?" line show, with the blend the recipe uses (`recipeFlourBlend(style, 'custom', flourChosen ? flourBlend : undefined)`): Fine-tune had passed the unchosen default flour and read 72 % ("four +3 · type de farine +1"; "four" is the oven, not W) while the recipe was 71 %. A flour never chosen adds nothing; the hint names only what moved the number ("71 % here for your oven."). Sweep: 12,960 calculateRecipe cases byte-identical to main. Tests: `tests/flour-w-tolerance.test.cjs`, `tests/about-science.test.cjs`, `tests/mobile/flour-w-tolerance.spec.cjs`. A flour change re-times a yeast plan (22 Nov 2026 round 9, J: W 200/320/380 typed after the recipe kept the W 200 plan, because the flour step never told the unmounted planner): the Flour step's `onBlendChange` sets `planReplanPending` when the new blend's `recipeFlourStrength` differs from the page's `flourStrength`, for a yeast dough only (a levain plan does not follow the flour); Update recipe then replans and says "Plan updated". In the engine the tolerance already acts wherever the window has room (direct Neapolitan, 22 °C, bake 72 h ahead: Start Dough Sat 9:10pm / 8:10pm / 6:10pm / 12:10pm / 10:10am at 0.75 / 0.9 / 1 / 1.25 / 1.35; 24 h ahead the window caps it and every flour gets the same plan). ### Schedule
Single-phase, or two-phase (bulk spell, shape, shaped spell) when the window holds bulk + 2 h cold (4 h below 28 °C) + division + 2 h shaped cold + 1 h proof + preheat (about 6.5 h at 30 °C: the old "≥16 h" note was stale).
15-min rounding · busy-time edges by rule E1 (below) · default fridge 6 °C · default bake 18h.
**Busy-time edges, rule E1 (owner, 25 Nov 2026, round 21).** A busy time is when the baker cannot touch the dough. QUICK hands-on steps (a few minutes: fold, into/out of the fridge, take-out, preheat on, starter into/out of the fridge; `isQuickAction`) may sit exactly on a busy time's edge, at its start ("before bed") or its end ("on waking"). LONG hands-on steps (Start Dough and its finish, autolyse, Make Poolish/Biga, a feed with mixing, divide & shape, the bake) must END by its start (a span's end is exclusive) and may start at its end; a long step known only by its start counts as inside when it starts at the busy time's start. Everything inside stays forbidden. ONE function, `busyHit(action, from, to)` in `app/utils/scheduleAvailability.ts` (`isTimeBlocked(t, blocks, quick)`, `inClockWindow` for clock windows), read by every checker: `findAvailabilityConflicts` (clash sheets, `assessScheduleDraft`, the editor), the night rules with 22:00–07:00 (and 23:00–06:00, `isDeepNight`; 23:00–07:00, `isOvernightPreparation`) read as busy times (`newNightHandsOn`, utils.ts `nightActionIds`, `nightHandsOn`, `nightScore`: the entry-plan ranking and the night line), the engine's exit/fridge-return scans (`divideIsBlocked`, `coldExitConflict`, shortest warm-up, bake from the fridge), the sourdough solver's fridge moves and revival chain (`inBlockerQuickMs`, `candidateQuickTimes`), the plan rows' busy tag and Preparation's clash line. ### DDT
The water-temperature balance includes flour, starter or preferment, thermal input from kneading, and the heat of melting ice. A levain's flour and water are already inside the dough's totals; in the balance they sit at the starter's temperature: room temperature after a feed, the warmed temperature (`prefTempAfterWarmup` from fridge to kitchen over the time out) when a fridge starter goes into the dough without another feed, or the baker's measured temperature (Fine-tune), which always wins (`prefTempForBalance` in page.tsx). Use the calculated result for the specific formula. In Simple, kneading is assumed by hand and never mentioned on the recipe (12 Nov 2026: the mixer question under Water was removed; a mixer would add only a few degrees of friction, and Custom exposes equipment explicitly). The water line (`waterGuideLine`) says how to reach the temperature without a thermometer — room temperature, a fridge/room split, lukewarm, or ice — and offers "how to get it ▾" only when there are steps (ice, or a measured water source).
