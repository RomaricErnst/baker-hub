## Calculation and display conventions

### Commercial yeast
One model (`app/utils/yeastModel.ts`, 19 Nov 2026; method, data, constants and residuals in `docs/YEAST-MODEL.md`) sets every commercial-yeast dose and timing: direct dough, cold retard, hot kitchens, poolish/biga, the inverse for a baker-entered amount and the poolish peak. Instant yeast % of the fermenting flour = A · M · R · Heq^−b, A 1.41, b 1.15 (Phase 3 refit, 20 Nov 2026: 157 rows, 28 lineages, mixed effects with per-source offsets, credibility × regime weights; `docs/yeast-model/fit3/`); Heq = ∫ r(T(t)) dt, equivalent hours at 25 °C over the dough's temperature path (`doughThermalPath` in utils.ts: leaves the bowl at the target dough temperature, relaxes to the kitchen, cools into the fridge as the bulk or as pieces, warms back; τ from `thermalTauH`); r = Rosso CTMI relative to 25 °C with Tmax 45.4 (Salvadó 2011), Topt 40 (fitted, at its bound) and Tmin −3.2 (fitted; LOW CONFIDENCE); M = 1 salted dough, 0.70 poolish, 1.55 biga (fitted); R = rise target, 1 (doubled, E1), easing to 0.76 when the dough ends between 25 and 28 °C (E3, fitted). E2: the fridge mostly holds (r(6 °C) ≈ 0.11), the dough finishes at room temperature. It replaced Craig's RT law, the separate cold law, the fridge Q10, the tropical ÷1.15/÷1.25, the poolish ÷3.1/×1.65, biga ×2.2 and the leavening top-up. No salt or hydration factor (no source); the enriched rule (≥10 % sugar → fixed 1 % IDY) and the >5 % sugar osmotic note are kept as implemented; fresh ×3, active dry ×1.33 (MB6 KM p. xi). The constants change only with a refit (`docs/yeast-model/fit3/`, pre-registered registry and hold-out) and `tests/yeast-model.test.cjs` (constants ≡ doc ≡ About page, data-fit bounds, smoothness 2–40 °C / 1–96 h / fridge 1–10 °C, reference recipes). Distinguish extending a bake with cold time from replacing room-temperature time at a fixed bake time: the budget is NOT monotone in the window (a plan long enough to take the cold retard ferments less than a slightly shorter room-temperature one). Display the converted amount for the selected yeast. The 0.5 g threshold flags weighing difficulty; the automatic dose stays within 0.05–1.5 % IDY before an explicit override.

### Flour strength (21 Nov 2026, owner)
Hydration follows the flour TYPE only (`FLOUR_DATA.hydrationDelta`); W and protein never change it (W is an alveograph value at fixed water and does not predict absorption: Pasini Verde W295 58.8 % vs Marrone W390 58.7 %). Fermentation tolerance (`fermToleranceMultiplier`, the page's `flourStrength`) is the type's value, except for a white-wheat type (`W_TOLERANCE_FLOURS`: pizza00, strong00, bread, allpurpose, manitoba) whose W is STATED (`wIsStated`: w source 'manual', 'photo', or 'exact' = a product's published W): then `ftmFromW(W)` (data.ts; piecewise linear (130,0.75) (200,0.90) (250,1.00) (300,1.20) (360,1.35), flat outside, rounded to 0.01) REPLACES it (`flourTolerance`, never multiplied). Rye, wholemeal and semolina keep their type. Blends weigh each component's own tolerance; flour 1's W is `w1` only (a legacy `wOverride` may be a blended W and is never read as stated). Catalogue tiles (`flourBehaviour`): an unmapped type (Italian 00/0/1…) is pizza00 unless its W is published and ≥ 270 (strong00); an estimated W no longer crosses to strong00 (Caputo Pizzeria/Nuvola/Cuoco… → pizza00: tolerance 1.2 → 1.0, hydration −2). Saved blends keep their stored tile. The tolerance scales coldH/preferredColdH, the fridge-poolish window and the readiness plateau (SchedulePicker), the levain % (`starterBand × ftm` in `referenceStarterPct`/`sourdoughGuidance`: kept, see the 21 Nov 2026 round-8 report — unsourced magnitude, under review), the sourdough peak tolerance and the bulk cue text. It does NOT touch the commercial yeast at a fixed schedule: the ÷ tolerance in `calculateRecipe` and the × tolerance on an entered amount (`yeastTimingFor`, `checkYeastEntry`) are gone (double count; `docs/YEAST-MODEL.md` §8.4). Texts: About "The flour", "Why this value?" (flour type + one line on W), the manual flour form. Tests: `tests/flour-w-tolerance.test.cjs`, `tests/about-science.test.cjs`, `tests/mobile/flour-w-tolerance.spec.cjs`.

### Schedule
Single-phase, or two-phase tropical when window ≥16 h AND kitchen ≥28 °C.
15-min rounding · blocker edges EXCLUSIVE (> and <, never >= <=) · default fridge 6 °C · default bake 18h.
Baguette apprêt (20 Nov 2026 round 7, D4; `docs/YEAST-MODEL.md` §13): a style with `shapedProofH24` in utils.ts STYLE_FERM_DEFAULTS (baguette 1.5) proofs its shaped pieces at most that many hours at 24 °C, converted by r(T) and rounded to the quarter (2 h to the bake at 20 °C, 2.5 h at 18, 3 h at 16, the 1 h 45 minimum from 22 °C; Hamelman, Grands Moulins de Paris, Bouabsa, King Arthur Pro), after the fridge and without one (`styleMaxFinalProofHours`: minus the preheat, ≥ 1 h before it, ≤ the generic `maxFinalProofHours`, which was 3.5 h/5 h at 20 °C). The exit moves later, the cold absorbs the time, the yeast is re-solved on the path. Two owner limits: `buildSchedule` builds main's plan first (generic bound, `FinalProofMode` 'generic') and keeps the style's apprêt only if (1) a plan with no cold phase on main stays at room temperature (`styleRoom`: the time goes to the bulk) and (2) no hands-on step newly starts 22:00–07:00 (`nightActionIds`; allowed where main had that step there, or outside every busy time when the baker entered their own ' night' block), with no extra clash or newly invalid preparation; it tries the split cold ('style'), then the whole cold before shaping ('styleBulk'), else main's plan. `finalProofBound: 'style'` marks a plan the style bound planned. Levain and every other style keep the generic bound; coldH/rtH unchanged (no three-engine change). Sweep (TZ=UTC): 659 of 17,550 plans change, all baguette; 0 gain a clash, 0 newly night steps, 0 room plans gain cold. Tests: `tests/baguette-final-proof.test.cjs`.
Final fridge exit (two-phase, 18 Nov 2026 retention round 4): free plans keep the engine's exit (longest final proof; sweep: 0 of 10,780 no-blocker plans changed). (1) When the exit must fall in busy time, it is the LATEST workable one (warm-up + shortest final proof before the preheat, e.g. 6:45 for a 9:15 bake after nights to 7:00), or the first free quarter if one exists; an exit the baker arranged for this exact bake (`fridgeRemoval` {at, bake}) is kept, so saved bakes are never re-timed. (2) Bake again carries the previous plan's exit-to-bake lead (`planExitLead` → session `exitLead`, `app/utils/exitLead.ts`, same kitchen temp and style only) as `buildSchedule(…, exitLeadMin)`: used only when that exit is free and inside [longest, shortest] proof bounds, so a later bake extends the cold spell instead of the proof (Thomas: 11:00 bake → out 8:00, 3 h as last week). Every planner input (`ScheduleDraftInput`, `EditInput`, repair, yeast inversion, bake-from-fridge) passes it. (3) A longer preheat chosen on the plan (`preheatExtraMin`) moves only the Preheat step: the dough is planned with `planPreheatMin` (the oven's own) and `withLongerPreheat` moves the preheat action (page schedule and `assessScheduleDraft`). The "Before your recipe" sheet states a clash and another live bake's oven time (`ovenShare`, plural subject from `bakeSubject`) in one sentence; its suggestions avoid that oven time and Keep also keeps the oven (`onOvenKept`). ### DDT
The water-temperature balance includes flour, starter or preferment, thermal input from kneading, and the heat of melting ice. A levain's flour and water are already inside the dough's totals; in the balance they sit at the starter's temperature: room temperature after a feed, the warmed temperature (`prefTempAfterWarmup` from fridge to kitchen over the time out) when a fridge starter goes into the dough without another feed, or the baker's measured temperature (Fine-tune), which always wins (`prefTempForBalance` in page.tsx). Use the calculated result for the specific formula. In Simple, kneading is assumed by hand and never mentioned on the recipe (12 Nov 2026: the mixer question under Water was removed; a mixer would add only a few degrees of friction, and Custom exposes equipment explicitly). The water line (`waterGuideLine`) says how to reach the temperature without a thermometer — room temperature, a fridge/room split, lukewarm, or ice — and offers "how to get it ▾" only when there are steps (ice, or a measured water source).

### Preferments
Poolish RT 6–14 h, fridge ≤24 h · Biga fridge ≤72 h · Levain = sourdough only.
Preferment doses (yeast model, 19 Nov 2026): a poolish or biga holds the peak dose of its OWN path (`prefermentPath` in data.ts: made at the kitchen temperature; a fridge poolish out for its warm-up before the mix, a room poolish chilled for its cool-down; medium factor M), capped 0.05–1.5 %; the final dough gets its own yeast, the direct-dough need of the dough's path from the mix to the bake (`finalYeastGrams`, always present with a preferment; Modernist's poolish doughs total 0.4–1.9× that need, geometric mean 1.16; no credit for the preferment's yeast; preferments above ~35 % of the flour are outside that evidence). `recipe.yeast` is that final-dough need (the old `yeastReduction` is no longer applied). A made preferment stays valid when only the dough's path moves (`madeFormulaMatches` compares the preferment). Poolish near its peak at the mix (18 Nov 2026, owner; Thomas, 20 °C, 11 h poolish held the whole dough's yeast and peaked ~4.5 h in): the test is the model read backwards (`poolishPeakHours` inverts the peak dose of `computePrefermentRecipe` on the same path; pass `massG` and the conditioning hours): a poolish dosed for its window peaks at the mix by construction, so the levers below act only when the dose is held at the 0.05 % floor or the window outruns the dosed one (`preferment.pastPeak`, which the page's `poolishPeakAtMix` reads). Past its peak when it peaks more than `poolishPastPeakToleranceH` before the mix: usable until peak + max(`rtTolUpper` 0.75–2 h, ⅓ of its time to peak) at room temperature (fond.kitchen: a 12 h poolish is "past peak but usable" to 14–16 h; Suas's 12–15 h span), peak + 12 h in the fridge (The Dough Formula; fond: a ripe poolish holds ~24 h cold) (19 Nov 2026 recheck; the books give a visual cue only, no hours). Only then, levers the baker left to the planner (`app/utils/poolishRipeness.ts`, judged by the page's `poolishPeakAtMix` = the recipe the plan would show): (2) fridge, only with storage 'auto', in the fridge protocol's window (≤24 h, warm-up kept); (3) a later poolish start, same mix, only without a set poolish time, within the storage's own window (never below it); (4) else the plan stands (the final dough's yeast line is always there; whole-dough row, shopping and mixing batches use `recipeYeastTotal`). The poolish % is never changed. Plans that pass are returned as found (no extra fields). ### Recipe order (20 Nov 2026 round 7, owner)
Professional formula order (Hamelman, King Arthur: overall formula → preferment → final dough), in both modes: the **whole dough** first (`data-whole-dough`, heading "Whole dough" / « Pâte complète »; a direct dough keeps "Dough ingredients" and has no stages), ending with Total dough; then a visually smaller breakdown by stage (`data-recipe-stages`, `recipeStages` in `app/utils/recipeStages.ts`): Poolish/Biga (flour named by its flour, water at room temperature, yeast) and Final dough ("All the poolish", each flour, water with the water line, salt, yeast, oil, sugar); a levain is "Levain build" / « Rafraîchi du levain » (seed from the jar, flour, water for the 1:R:R feed, `starterBuild`) and Final dough ("Levain" with "keep the rest (N g)", each flour less the starter's, water less the starter's, salt). No "Of which prefermented flour", no blend sentence, no stage totals, no "Remaining". The stages add up to the whole dough ingredient by ingredient (`levainSplit`: the starter's flour is half rounded, its water the rest). With several flours and a preferment or levain the whole dough has one Flour line at 100 % with each flour beneath in grams only (the first flour carries the preferment's, so its share differs from the chosen blend; no % that would read as a contradiction). Stage yeast (`stageYeastDoses`): kitchen-scale figures, and where the stages would not add up to the whole dough's figure the fewest doses get one more figure (2 significant figures under 1 g, 2 decimals 1–10 g: 0.45 + 0.7 → 1.2 g); when no choice adds up, the whole dough is shown as their sum with the same extra figure.
